# Outage early warning: evaluation

Generated by `python -m evops.early_warning evaluate`.

**Task.** For every station that is working at a cutoff date, predict whether it will report an
unexpected outage (a charge point out of order or inoperative, seen in at least 2 three-hour
blocks) in the next 7 days. Scheduled closures, such as supermarket chargers switched off at night
and on Sundays, are learned per station and don't count. All features use only the 21 days
before the cutoff.

## Conclusion

LightGBM ranks best in both tests, but only slightly ahead of a simple rule: rank stations by how often they failed in the last 21 days (AUC 0.847 vs 0.840 in August, 0.870 vs 0.857 in September). Repeated recent faults are by far the strongest signal, and first-time failures are barely predictable (AUC 0.576). The practical value is a ranked list of stations with intermittent faults: of the 100 stations ranked riskiest, 100% (August) and 96% (September) had an outage within a week, against a base rate of 16% and 25%.

**Training data.** 129,453 station-cutoffs from 6 cutoff dates, 21 to 31 July 2026.

## Test 1: the following week (cutoff 7 Aug 2026, 23,053 stations, 16% had an outage)

| Model | AUC | Avg. precision | Precision, top 100 | Precision, top 500 | Recall, top 500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rule: share of time down, last 21 days | 0.811 | 0.418 | 38% | 42% | 6% |
| Rule: how often it failed, last 21 days | 0.840 | 0.630 | 99% | 94% | 13% |
| Logistic regression | 0.829 | 0.627 | 98% | 95% | 13% |
| LightGBM | 0.847 | 0.657 | 100% | 95% | 13% |

## Test 2: September, a different capture regime (37,214 station-weeks from 3 cutoffs, 25% had an outage)

The model is still trained only on July data. In September the archive recorded each station only every
~2.5 hours instead of every few minutes, and coverage dropped. Features are built on a 3-hour grid so
both periods look alike to the model.

| Model | AUC | Avg. precision | Precision, top 100 | Precision, top 500 | Recall, top 500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rule: share of time down, last 21 days | 0.845 | 0.619 | 52% | 51% | 3% |
| Rule: how often it failed, last 21 days | 0.857 | 0.733 | 94% | 97% | 5% |
| Logistic regression | 0.868 | 0.745 | 94% | 97% | 5% |
| LightGBM | 0.870 | 0.749 | 96% | 94% | 5% |

## What drives the predictions

Permutation importance on test 1: how much AUC drops when one signal is shuffled.

| Signal | AUC lost when shuffled |
|---|---|
| New outages started per 100 observations, last 21 days | 0.112 |
| Operator's network-wide outage share, last 7 days | 0.010 |
| Share of time with an outage, last 21 days | 0.009 |
| Days since last outage | 0.008 |
| Charge points at the station | 0.007 |
| Maximum power (kW) | 0.004 |
| Change in usage, last 7 vs 21 days | 0.002 |
| Share of time with an outage, last 7 days | 0.001 |

## How to read the numbers

- **AUC**: chance that a random station that did fail is ranked above one that didn't (0.5 = random, 1 = perfect).
- **Precision, top 100**: of the 100 stations ranked riskiest, the share that had an outage that week.
  This is what a maintenance team with capacity for 100 checks a week would see.
- **Recall, top 500**: share of all stations that failed that week that were in the top 500.
